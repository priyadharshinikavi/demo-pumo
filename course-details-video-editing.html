Video Editing Syllabus

Adobe Premiere Pro & After Effects • 3 Months Program
3 Months

Module 1: Adobe Premiere Pro (Video Editing & Audio Cleanup)

Learn timeline workflows, advanced cuts, keyframe animations, audio cleanup, color correction, and final project outputs.

1. Introduction to Adobe Premiere Pro Premiere Pro Basics

What is video editing. What is Video Formats? What is Codec? What is Frame Rates? Adobe Premiere Pro software installation. Overview of panels. Creating and saving a new project. Open Premiere Pro, explore interface, and create a new project.

2. Project Panel, Source Monitor, Timeline Panel Media Management

Importing media, creating bins, organizing files. Trimming clips, In/Out points, Insert & Overwrite edits. Sequence creation, track controls, markers, snapping. Create a project and organize imported media. Trim and insert clips into the timeline. Arrange clips in the timeline with markers.

3. Editing Tools Timeline Tools

Playback controls, basic editing tools (Selection, Ripple, Rolling, Razor, Slip, Slide, Pen, Hand, Zoom). Perform edits using Razor and Ripple Edit tools.

4. Effect Controls, Keyframes Animations

Motion properties (Position, Scale, Rotation, Anchor Point). Opacity and Blend Modes. What are keyframes. Temporal interpolation types (Linear, Bezier, Ease In/Out). Apply a scale & position animation to a clip. Animate text opacity using keyframes.

5. Effects & Transitions, Nesting & Speed Changes Speed & FX

Video & audio effects, transitions, and rendering. Nesting sequences. Speed/duration changes, reverse, and speed ramping. Apply cross dissolve and directional blur between two clips. Create a slow-motion effect with speed ramping.

6. Audio Editing, Audio Cleanup Audio Tools

Audio track types (Mono vs Stereo). Audio levels, transitions, and presets. Denoise and audio channel adjustments. Apply constant power transition between two audio clips. Remove background noise from a dialogue clip.

7. Color Correction & Color Grading Color Correction

Difference between color correction & grading. Lumetri Color & Lumetri Scopes. Creative color adjustments and LUTs. Color formats (Rec.709, LOG, RAW). Correct exposure and white balance in a video. Apply a cinematic look using color grading.

8. Titles & Graphics, Advanced Effects Graphics & VFX

Text Tool, Essential Graphics, lower thirds, end roll credits. Green screen (Ultra Key), crop, warp stabilizer, picture-in-picture. Create and animate a lower third text. Replace a green screen background.

9. Exporting Media Export Settings

Export formats (MP4, MOV, JPEG, TIFF, PNG, WAV, MP3). Export settings for different platforms. Export a video in H.264 and QuickTime formats.

10. Adobe Premiere Pro Final Project Project Task

At least 4–5 video clips or images. Use at least 2 different video, 5 different Image transitions. Intro title (project name), End title (thank you / credits), One background music track. Export the final video in MP4 format.

Module 2: Adobe After Effects (Motion Graphics & Visual Effects)

Explore layers, transformations, text animator presets, shapes, masking, keying, motion graphics, and composition rendering.

1. Introduction to After Effects AE Basics

What is After Effects? Difference between After Effects, Premiere Pro. After Effects software installation. Interface, workspace & tools overview. Creating a new project & composition. Importing images, video & audio. Open After Effects, explore interface, and Create a simple composition and import media.

2. Understanding Layers & Transformations Transform Properties

Understanding layers (Video, Text, Shape, Solid, Adjustment). Transform properties (Position, Scale, Rotation, Opacity, Anchor Point). Animate text or shape with keyframes.

3. Working with Keyframes & Animation Keyframes & Easing

Keyframes basics. Motion paths. Easing (Easy Ease, Graph Editor). Animate a text or shape moving across the screen.

4. Working with Text Animation Text Animators

Text layer properties. Preset text animations (Fade, Typewriter, Slide, Bounce). Create a title animation with text effect.

5. Shapes & Masks Masking

Shape layers vs Solid layers. Masks (Add, Subtract, Feather). Animate a shape reveal using masks.

6. Effects & Presets AE Effects

Applying Effects (Blur, Glow, Color Correction). Using Presets for quick animations. Add glow to text, blur background, and color correct a video.

7. Motion Graphics Basics Motion Graphics

Introduction to Motion Graphics. Using shape layers for design. Looping animation. Create a simple logo and icon animation.

8. Green Screen & Blending Keying

Chroma Keying (Keylight effect). Blending modes & opacity. Remove a green screen background. Blend two video layers creatively.

9. Rendering & Exporting Rendering

Render Queue vs Adobe Media Encoder. Export settings (MP4, MOV, GIF).

10. After Effects Final Project Intro Animation

Compile a 3-minute animated intro screen applying motion graphics, layers, easing, masks, keyframes, and blending effects.
